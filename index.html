About

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Eskisehir Technical University, Turkiye. My research focuses on estimation theory and its applications to rail system vehicles — including adhesion and friction estimation at the wheel–rail interface, dynamic weigh-in-motion systems, and intelligent condition monitoring.

I received my Ph.D. from the University of Pardubice (Czech Republic) in 2017, where I worked on estimation of states and parameters from the dynamic response of a wheelset. Between 2019 and 2020, I was a Research Associate with the Future Mobility Research Group at Newcastle University (UK), contributing to the Horizon 2020 project ETALON and the Active Radial Suspension System (ARSS) project.

I also serve as an Assistant Editor of the journal Railway Engineering (TR DIZIN), published by the Association of Railway Engineers.
